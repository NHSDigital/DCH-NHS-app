Your baby's first year vaccinations

Vaccination protects your baby against serious illnesses. Having vaccinations on time gives your baby the best possible protection.

Your GP surgery will usually contact you when your baby is due for a vaccine.

If you have not received an invite, or if your baby has missed any doses, contact your GP surgery.

Vaccines your baby needs in their first year

8 weeks

  • 6-in-1 vaccine (1st dose).
  • MenB vaccine (1st dose).
  • Rotavirus vaccine (1st dose).

12 weeks

  • 6-in-1 vaccine (2nd dose).
  • MenB vaccine (2nd dose).
  • Rotavirus vaccine (2nd dose).

16 weeks

  • 6-in-1 vaccine (3rd dose).
  • Pneumococcal vaccine (1st dose).

1 year

  • MMRV (measles, mumps, rubella and chickenpox) vaccine (1st dose for babies born on or after 1 January 2025).
  • Pneumococcal vaccine (2nd dose).
  • MenB vaccine (3rd dose).

Babies who may need extra vaccinations

Some babies need extra protection, especially those who:

  • were born prematurely.
  • have a long-term health condition.
  • are at higher risk of infections like hepatitis B or tuberculosis (TB).

Your midwife, health visitor or GP will let you know if your baby needs any of these extra vaccines.
