As part of the NHS’ support to babies, parents, carers and families, you will receive a series of messages relevant to the age of your baby.

These messages are sent via the NHS App. They offer trusted information on topics like: 

  • How to register for a GP
  • What to expect from a health visitor appointment
  • About your 6-8 week baby and mother checkups
  • When to introduce solid food
  • Vitamins for your baby
