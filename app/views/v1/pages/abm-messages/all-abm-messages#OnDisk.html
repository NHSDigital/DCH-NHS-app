Health Visitor

Your health visitor appointment is coming up.

A health visitor is a qualified nurse or midwife who has had extra training. They're there to help you and your new baby.

Every parent is seen by a health visitor. They will normally visit you at your home around 10 days after your baby is born.

If you have not been contacted about an appointment contact your health visiting team or your midwife.

You do not need to wait for this appointment if you are worried about something.

Contact your GP practice straight away if you have any concerns.

This is a trial of a new service. If you would like to opt out of receiving these messages please visit https://www.nhs.uk/your-nhs-data-matters/manage-your-choice/

Vitamins

Vitamins help your baby's healthy development. The NHS recommends breastfed babies have vitamin D supplements from birth. All children aged 6 months to 5 years should be given vitamin supplements containing vitamins A, C and D every day (unless they are having 500ml or more of infant formula.)

Be careful not to double dose. Having too many of some vitamins can be harmful.

If your child has already been prescribed vitamins, consult your health visitor, doctor or pharmacist.

For more advice about giving vitamins to children: nhs.uk/baby/weaning-and-feeding/vitamins-for-children/
