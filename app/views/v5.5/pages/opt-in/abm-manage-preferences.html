Parents and carers are automatically signed up for age-based messages when they have a baby. These are timed messages based on the age of your baby, reminding you of important information like appointments, weaning and vitamins.


You can only use this service to manage your preferences for Age-based Messages.

Before you start

To find your record, we'll need a few details.

If you know your NHS number:

  • Your NHS number
  • Your full name

If you don't know your NHS number:

  • Your full name
  • Your date of birth
  • Your postcode registered with your GP

We'll use these details to verify who you are so that you can manage your preferences.

How to find your NHS number

An NHS number is a 10 digit number, like 999 123 4567.

You can find your NHS number by logging in to the NHS App. From your Home screen, go to "Profile" and find your name. Your NHS number is shown under your name and date of birth. Most parents and carers can find their NHS number on recent NHS documents such as:

  • Maternity documents
  • Discharge papers
  • Appointment letters
  • Prescriptions

You can also use the Find your NHS number service.
