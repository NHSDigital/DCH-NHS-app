Register your baby with a GP

If you or your child get sick, a GP (General Practitioner) or doctors' surgery is usually your first point of contact.

They can help you if your baby gets ill or if you are worried about the baby's health. They can also refer you to other NHS services.

Your child can be registered at a different surgery to you.

Register for free

Everyone in England can register with a GP surgery, for free. You can also change your GP surgery for free.

How to register your baby

If you haven't registered already, you can register by:

  • using the NHS website or NHS app to register with a GP surgery
  • downloading the register with a GP surgery paper form, filling it in and taking it to the surgery
  • or going to the surgery

You can register with a GP even if you have not already registered the baby’s birth.
