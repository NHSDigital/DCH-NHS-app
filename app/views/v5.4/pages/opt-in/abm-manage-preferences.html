Before you start

To find your record, we'll need a few details.

If you know your NHS number:

  • Your NHS number
  • Your full name

If you don't know your NHS number

  • Your full name
  • Your date of birth
  • Your postcode registered with your GP
How to find your NHS number

An NHS number is a 10 digit number, like 999 123 4567.

You can find your NHS number by logging in to the NHS App under "My Profile". Most parents and carers can find their NHS number quickly on recent NHS documents such as:

  • maternity documents
  • discharge papers
  • appointment letters
  • prescriptions

You can also use the Find your NHS Number Service.

We'll use these details to verify who you are so that you can unsubscribe.
