You can use this service to unsubscribe from these messages.

Every parent/carer has been opted into essential age-based messages when they have a new born. These are timed messages based on the age of your baby, reminding you of essential information like appointments, weaning and vitamins.

Before you start

We'll need your:

  • Name
  • Date of birth
  • Postcode or NHS number

We'll use these details to verify who you are so that you can unsubscribe.

How to find your NHS number

An NHS number is a 10 digit number, like 999 123 4567.

You can find your NHS number by logging in to the NHS App under "My Profile". Most parents and carers can find their NHS number quickly on recent NHS documents such as:

  • maternity documents
  • discharge papers
  • appointment letters
  • prescriptions

You can also use the Find your NHS Number Service.
