As part of the NHS support to babies, parents and families, you will receive a series of messages relevant to your baby's age.

These messages are sent via the NHS App or SMS. They offer trusted information on topics like: 

  • What to expect from a health visitor appointment
  • About your 6-8 week baby and mother checkups
  • When to introduce solid food
  • Vitamins for your baby
