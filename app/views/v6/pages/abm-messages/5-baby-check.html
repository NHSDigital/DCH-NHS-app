Your baby’s 6 to 8 week health check

This appointment happens with the GP 6 to 8 weeks after your child is born.

It's to check for any general problems with your baby.

This is so any potential issues can be identified and treated as early as possible.

A health visitor will also see you and baby separately to this GP appointment for a full health and development review.

What to expect

This check focuses on how your baby is developing including to make sure everything is progressing as expected.

It specifically looks for any issues with their:

  • eyes
  • heart
  • hips
  • testicles (balls), if they're a boy
  • growth and development

This helps to find any problems so they can be treated as early as possible.

Read more about your baby's 6-week check.

How to prepare for your baby consultation

To get the most from your consultation, it's helpful to:

  • think about or write down any concerns or questions you have about your baby's health, feeding, or development
  • bring your baby's red book (Personal Child Health Record)

If you are worried about something

You do not need to wait for this appointment if you are worried about something. Contact your GP practice if you have any concerns.

If you have not been contacted about an appointment

Contact your GP.
