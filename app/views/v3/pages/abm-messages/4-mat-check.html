Your 6 to 8 week maternal postnatal health consultation

This appointment happens 6 to 8 weeks after your child is born.

It is to support your physical health and mental wellbeing.

A health visitor will also see you and your baby separately to this GP appointment.

Preparing for your maternal consultation

To get the most from your appointment, it’s helpful to:

  • prepare a list of questions or concerns you may have
  • arrange for someone to come with you if you want extra support

What to expect

A trained healthcare professional will:

  • ask about your mental wellbeing
  • arrange for someone to be with you if you want extra support
  • ask about your birthing experience, especially if this was traumatic or unexpected
  • follow up on any health issues you may have developed during/after pregnancy
  • assess your pelvic health
  • ask how feeding is going and any concerns with breastfeeding or breast health
  • talk about contraception
  • review any pre-existing medical problems and any conditions related to your pregnancy

If you have not been contacted about an appointment

Contact your health visiting team or your midwife.
