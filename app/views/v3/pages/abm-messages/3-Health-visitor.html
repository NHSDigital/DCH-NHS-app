Your health visitor appointment

A health visitor is a qualified nurse or midwife who has had extra training.

They're there to help you, your family and your new baby.

Every parent is seen by a health visitor. They will normally visit you at your home.

They will visit you and your family around 10 days after your baby is born.

You will be contacted to confirm the appointment.

What to expect

Your health visitor can help you with things like:

  • bonding with your baby
  • your mental health
  • vaccinations and screening tests
  • feeding your baby

Assess your baby's development

You can talk about how things are going and ask any questions you have.

Prepare for your visit

To get the most from your appointment, it’s helpful to:

  • prepare a list of questions or concerns about your health or your baby’s development
  • arrange for someone to be with you if you want extra support

If you have not been contacted about an appointment

Contact your health visiting team or your midwife.
