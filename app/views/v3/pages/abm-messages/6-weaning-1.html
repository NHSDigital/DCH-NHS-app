When to introduce solid foods

Don't begin weaning too early

Introducing solid foods is recommended by the NHS when babies are around 6 months old.

You may hear people say they started at 4 months, however the NHS recommends waiting to around 6 months when a baby is able to:

  • stay in a sitting position, holding their head steady
  • coordinate their eyes, hands and mouth so they can look at their food, pick it up and put it in their mouth
  • swallow food (rather than spit it back out)
  • At this stage, babies need more than milk alone. Solid foods provide extra energy and nutrients to support healthy growth and development.

This process of introducing solid foods is sometimes called 'complementary feeding' or 'weaning'.

When you begin this may vary from baby to baby but should start around 6 months.
