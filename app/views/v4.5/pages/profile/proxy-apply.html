Use this service to send an application to your GP surgery for review. Your GP will check the other person approves your access, if they can understand this.

You need to be at the same GP surgery as the person you want to help.

Before you start

Information:

Discuss setting up this access with the person you want to help, if they can understand this.

You'll need to give details of the person you'd like to help. We'll ask for their:

  • name
  • date of birth
  • NHS number or postcode (using their NHS number is the best way for us to identify them)

If you're applying to help someone aged 16 or over, we'll email them your answers.
