What happens next

Before giving you access, the GP surgery may need to check:

  • proof of parental responsibility, for example a birth certificate
  • with anyone else that shares parental responsibility with you
  • for any safeguarding issues

This will usually be done in a few days, but may take up to a month.

Contact the GP surgery if you have any questions about your application.

How to access GP services

Once your GP surgery has set up access, you will automatically be able to use GP services for your child.

If this access is set up, you can use the NHS App or log in to the NHS website.

When your access will stop

Most GP surgeries have an automatic cut-off age, between 11-14, to protect young people's confidentiality.

If your online access ends, you will still be able to manage your child's health and care at the GP surgery in the same way you do now.

You can ask your GP surgery to restore access.

Your GP surgery will usually check the child agrees to your access (consents) before they restore it, unless there is a reason why the child cannot understand and make an informed decision (called lacking capacity.)
